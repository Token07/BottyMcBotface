# Privacy Policy for Botty

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## 1. Information We Collect

Botty processes only the information necessary to provide its features and operate reliably. This may include:

* **Discord Identifiers:** Discord user IDs, guild (server) IDs, channel IDs, message IDs, role IDs, and display names.
* **Command Usage:** Information about commands executed, including any arguments required to process the command.
* **Message Content:** Message content that is processed to provide requested features, moderation functionality, or other bot services.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed solely for the following purposes:

* Providing Botty's features and services.
* Performing automated and manual moderation functions.
* Processing commands and generating requested responses.
* Diagnosing, troubleshooting, and resolving technical issues.
* Maintaining the security, stability, and integrity of the bot.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 3. Data Storage

Most data is processed temporarily in volatile memory (RAM) while commands are executed. Certain operational and moderation data is stored persistently where necessary for Botty's functionality.

## 4. Data Retention

Data is retained according to its operational purpose.

### Retained Indefinitely

The following information is currently retained until manually removed:

* Member join and leave events.
* Message edit logs.
* Message deletion logs.
* Associated Discord identifiers required to link these events.

### Diagnostic Data

System logs, error reports, and other diagnostic information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate, resolve, and prevent technical issues affecting Botty.

Retention periods may change as Botty evolves. This policy will be updated if significant changes are made.

## 5. Data Access and Sharing

Access to collected data is restricted to authorized personnel.

* **Moderation Logs:** Moderation information posted to designated Discord channels is accessible only to Gurus and Administrators of the **Riot Games Third Party Developer Community** Discord server.
* **System Logs and Stored Data:** Persistent data and system logs are accessible only to the current operators and maintainers of Botty.

We do not disclose your data to third parties except as described in this policy or where required by applicable law.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Some commands require the use of third-party APIs to provide requested functionality.

When this occurs:

* Only the minimum amount of message content necessary to fulfill the request is transmitted.
* We do not intentionally transmit Discord user IDs, guild IDs, profiles, or unrelated message history unless required for the requested feature.
* Third-party services process data according to their own privacy policies, which are outside our control.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of data where applicable.

If you have questions about the data Botty stores or wish to request its removal, please contact the Botty operators through the **Riot Games Third Party Developer Community** Discord server.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Botty after an updated policy becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

## 9. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or Botty's data practices, please contact the Botty operators through the **Riot Games Third Party Developer Community** Discord server.
